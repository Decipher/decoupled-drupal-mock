A third preset holds Drupal CMS 2.2.9 with its Haven site template, recorded from a real install with Drupal Canvas and Canvas Headless. Haven is a climate non-profit's site, with blog posts, projects and a person profile, their images and tags. The preset answers Canvas pages and nodes through JSON:API and Canvas's content API, gives Canvas Headless's draft preview of its About us page, and serves itself with `npx decoupled-drupal-mock-haven@alpha --port 3006`.
