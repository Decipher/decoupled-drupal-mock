First release. The mock answers Drupal's JSON:API, Decoupled Router, menu and Simple OAuth requests from a preset recorded from a real Drupal. It runs inside tests, through msw or as an HTTP server, with entry points for Jest, Vitest, Playwright and Cypress, and takes extensions for what a preset does not have.
