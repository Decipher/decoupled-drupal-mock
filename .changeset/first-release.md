First release: Drupal's decoupled APIs without a Drupal site. JSON:API, Decoupled Router, menus and Simple OAuth are answered from a preset built from a real Drupal, in process, through msw or over HTTP, with entry points for Jest, Vitest, Playwright and Cypress, extensions for what a preset does not have, and overlays a site records of its own contrib modules and patches, aligned to the preset from any install of the same site.
