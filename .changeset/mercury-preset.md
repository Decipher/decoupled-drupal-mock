A fourth preset holds Drupal CMS 2.2.9 with its Mercury Demo site template, recorded from a real install with Drupal Canvas and Canvas Headless. Mercury Demo is the theme's own showcase: Canvas pages built from Mercury's components, such as its cards, sections, headings, buttons and calls to action. The preset answers its pages through JSON:API and Canvas's content API, gives Canvas Headless's draft preview of its Home page, and serves itself with `npx decoupled-drupal-mock-mercury@alpha --port 3007`.
