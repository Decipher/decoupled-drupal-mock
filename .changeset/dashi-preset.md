A second preset holds Drupal CMS 2.2.9 with its Dashi site template, recorded from a real install with Drupal Canvas and Canvas Headless. Dashi brings Umami's recipes and articles, in English and Spanish, with their images, tags and authors. The preset answers Canvas pages and nodes through JSON:API and Canvas's content API, and serves itself with `npx decoupled-drupal-mock-dashi@alpha --port 3005`.
